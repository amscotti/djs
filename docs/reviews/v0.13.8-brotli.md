# v0.13.8 — Brotli and fetch content decoding

## Plan

Fetch against CDNs and APIs must survive `content-encoding: br`/`gzip`, and
`zlib` should expose Node's Brotli helpers. This closes the decoding half of
Tier 1 item 10; DNS breadth remains open and is recorded in the roadmap.

## Architecture

- Native `BrotliEncoderCompress` / `BrotliDecoderDecompress` bindings in
  `src/djs/apis/zlib.d`, linked against `libbrotlienc.so.1` /
  `libbrotlidec.so.1` (the dev symlinks are absent on this host).
- Decoder grows its output buffer until `BROTLI_DECODER_RESULT_SUCCESS`, capped
  at 256 MiB.
- Prelude exposes `brotliCompressSync` (quality from
  `params[BROTLI_PARAM_QUALITY]`) and `brotliDecompressSync`.
- `Response._consume` runs `decodeContent` for `gzip`, `deflate` and `br`.
  Compressed responses are buffered, so SSE over compression is not
  incremental.

## Application review

| # | Severity | Finding | Reproduction | Fix |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | high | no Brotli | `brotliCompressSync` undefined | native bindings |
| 2 | high | fetch returned compressed bytes | `fetch(...).text()` garbled with `br` | decode on consume |
| 3 | medium | link failed: no `libbrotli*.so` dev symlinks | `-lbrotlienc` | link the `.so.1` sonames |
| 4 | medium | `JS_GetArrayBuffer` rejects Buffer views | `brotliCompress expects a Buffer` | typed-array acquisition helper |
| 5 | low | quality constant missing | `zlib.constants.BROTLI_PARAM_QUALITY` undefined | added |
